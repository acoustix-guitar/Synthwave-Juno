A polyphonic synthesizer for REAPER in classic Juno-60 style. Warm pads, punchy basses and neon leads for Synthwave, Retrowave and 80s music.

Everything you expect from a Juno

The panel follows the original layout, so all parameters are visible at once. No menus, no hidden pages.

Oscillator & filter

  • Saw, pulse/PWM, sub and noise
  • 24 dB resonant low-pass filter
  • 4-position high-pass filter

Modulation

  • ADSR envelope with live display
  • LFO: triangle, square, saw, S&H
  • Delay, auto/manual trigger

Chorus

Chorus I, II and I + II for the famous lush, wide Juno vibrato chorus.

Arpeggiator

Up, Up/Down and Down over 1–3 octaves, locked to the project tempo from 1/4 to 1/32, including triplets. Hold function included.

Playability

  • Poly, Mono and Legato
  • Portamento, key transpose
  • Velocity, pitch bend, mod wheel, sustain

Readable interface

Large fonts, scalable window, live filter response, envelope, LFO and scope displays.

48 factory presets

Install in a minute

Pure JSFX: no installer, no dependencies. Works with REAPER 6 or newer on Windows, macOS and Linux.

  1. Unzip the package and copy the folders Effects, Scripts and Data into your REAPER resource folder (Options > Show REAPER resource path).
  2. Restart REAPER and add JS: Synthwave Juno 60 to a track.
  3. Pick a sound from the preset bar and play.
  4. Own banks: put TAL-U-No-LX patches into Data\Synthwave_Juno\<Bank>, run Juno_Build_Presets.lua once and restart REAPER.
<REAPER resource path>\
|-- Effects\Synth\
|     |-- Juno_Synthwave_Synth.jsfx
|     `-- Juno_Synthwave_Synth.jsfx.rpl
|-- Scripts\Juno_Build_Presets.lua
`-- Data\Synthwave_Juno\
      |-- Bass\  |-- Keys\  |-- Lead\
      `-- Pluck\ |-- Pad\   `-- FX\
